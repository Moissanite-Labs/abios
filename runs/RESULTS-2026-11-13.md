## Readings, per the prereg's committed rule

Knowledge reading. At least one configuration moves materially on comprehension, or at least one compile comes out validator-valid. Both hold, in every configuration, for both models.

On these ten tasks, with the grammar in context, both models score 19 of 31 on the registered v2 total against 4 to 6 of 31 cold. Cold read was not zero: cold v2 comprehension was 4 to 6 of 23, and the cold t09 compile differed from the reference in one field. The six configurations also share 10 of 16 answer slots byte-for-byte, with low and medium often matching in usage too (caveat 2), so the six rows are not six independent measurements. The receiver-with-spec shape looks viable on this corpus; this run does not establish more than that.

Compile deserves the closer look. Cold read produced 0 of 2 validator-valid packets. With the full spec in context, every configuration produced 2 of 2 validator-valid packets, one of which (t09) is byte-exact against the reference.

t10 is valid but not exact, the EVAL.md category of grasping the grammar but not the canonical form. The t09 packet does not appear verbatim in `SPEC-V2-DRAFT.md`. The cold runs already emitted the same body with BASE `0` instead of empty (`|1|0|0` versus `|1||0`). Section 2 says BASE "empty means none" (`SPEC-V2-DRAFT.md:47`), and that sentence is outside the sections 3–4 excerpt pasted into the cold compile prompt. The new exactness is that one field.

Several task items are the spec's own worked examples: the t01/t07 line is `SPEC-V2-DRAFT.md:202`; the t03/t08 body and its gloss ("let cov be 62. If cov is less than 70, implement section 6.") are `SPEC-V2-DRAFT.md:273-276`; the t04 body and "implement section s" are `SPEC-V2-DRAFT.md:283-287`; the t05 body and "sections 7 to 9" are `SPEC-V2-DRAFT.md:307-311`. The render move from 0/6 to 1/6 is that section 11.1 gloss (astra-low t08: "Let cov be 62. If cov is less than 70, implement section 6; otherwise, do nothing."). The t02 and t10 bodies are not in the spec.

## Caveats, stated in plain terms

1. Serving path changed. The pilot ran flex through api.openai.com. This arm ran through the Portal lane at default tier. Model identity, corpus, prompts and scoring are unchanged. Serving stack and tier are disclosed here and in #50.
2. Cross-configuration uniformity. 10 of 16 (task, form) answer sets are byte-identical across all six configurations, including across models. A gateway-side caching layer cannot be excluded from outside.

Against that: call costs differ by model and effort, t01 answers vary in prose, and cold read on the same pinned prompts produced varied, wrong outputs. The pinned prompt design collapses diversity once both models actually know the answer.
3. This is ten hand-written tasks. It is a smoke result, not a benchmark, and nothing here generalizes past these six configurations. No model ranking is claimed. Astra and Sol are indistinguishable on this corpus under the strict scorer.
4. #33 remains open and was not updated by this work, although the preregistration said it would be. This arm narrows the comprehension harness question. It does not close the paper's multilingual arm or cross-linking.
5. Log provenance. `raw-responses.jsonl` stores only `task`, `form`, `text`, `ok`, `error` (`bench/run-api.py:243-247`); `usage.jsonl` has no request id, response model or timestamp, and `cached_tokens` is 0 on every call. `service_tier: flex` in `answers.json` is the CLI default recorded by `bench/run-api.py:228-229`; the chat body (`bench/run-api.py:105-114`) does not send a tier. That calls ran after the 03:35:51Z lane amendment on #50 rests on the issue comment times and commit dates, not on the logs.

```
OPENAI_API_WIRE=chat OPENAI_API_ENDPOINT=http://127.0.0.1:8645/v1/chat/completions \

  --model openai/gpt-6-astra --effort low --spec-in-context --max-cost 5

```

Requires the local Hermes proxy (`hermes proxy start --provider nous`) or any OpenAI-compatible chat completions endpoint serving the same model IDs. Raw responses and per-call usage are committed under `runs/2026-11-13-openai-*/`.

## What this supports in the paper

The claim the corpus supports is precise: on these ten tasks, at these six configurations, with the full v2 spec in the receiver's context, the registered v2 scorer total moves from 4 to 6 of 31 to 19 of 31 (comprehension-only slice: 4 to 6 of 23 to 18 of 23), and compile moves from 0 of 2 validator-valid to 2 of 2 validator-valid, one packet byte-exact and one field away from the cold output. Several items are the spec's own worked examples (see Readings). That is a direct measurement of the receiver-with-spec deployment shape, not of the language in the abstract.
